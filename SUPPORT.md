# Support — VA Record Snapshot (Chrome Extension)

Help, questions, bug reports, and privacy requests for the **VA Record Snapshot** Chrome
extension published by Task Force VA.

---

## Contact

**Email: support@taskforceva.io**

We read every message. Include your Chrome version and the extension version (shown in the
extension under Settings) if you are reporting a problem — it saves a round trip.

**Website:** https://taskforceva.ai

---

## Before you write in

### The extension says it needs consent, or nothing loads

Open the extension and accept the consent notice. Nothing is read from your VA record until
you do. If you previously declined, open the extension again and the notice will reappear.

### My snapshot disappeared

That is expected. **Your snapshot is held in memory only and is cleared when you close your
browser.** It is never written to your hard drive. Open the extension and select **Take my
snapshot** to collect a fresh one.

If you want a copy that persists, use the export option to save it to a file you control.

### It says I am not signed in to VA.gov

Sign in at https://www.va.gov in the same browser, then try again. The extension uses the
VA session you establish yourself — it never handles your VA.gov, Login.gov, or ID.me
credentials, and it cannot sign in for you.

### A section shows "unavailable"

That means VA's system did not return that section when the snapshot was taken. It does
**not** mean the section is empty or that a benefit was denied. Try again later.

### A rating shows "other — see your decision letter"

VA returned a decision value the extension does not recognize, so it says so rather than
guessing. Your decision letter is authoritative. We deliberately never report an
unrecognized value as a denial.

### I want my data deleted

- **On your device:** open the extension, go to **Settings > Privacy & Data**, and delete
  your snapshot. Uninstalling the extension also removes all locally stored extension data.
- **From your Task Force VA account**, if you chose to sync: email
  **support@taskforceva.io** and we will process the deletion.

## Reporting a security issue

If you believe you have found a security vulnerability in the extension, please email
**support@taskforceva.io** with "SECURITY" in the subject line rather than opening a public
issue. We will acknowledge and work the report directly.
